FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most commonly asked questions about admissions and programs.

What is the admission process at Vaishnavi College?
The admission process is simple: 1) Fill the enquiry form on our website or visit the campus, 2) Attend a counseling session with our academic advisor, 3) Submit required documents and confirm admission with the fee payment. You can also reach us via WhatsApp for instant assistance.
What is the eligibility criteria for different courses?
For BBA & B.Com Honours: Minimum 50% marks in 10+2 examination with English as one of the subjects. For BCA Honours: Minimum 50% in 10+2 with Mathematics/Computer Science. For B.Sc Computers: Minimum 50% in 10+2 with Mathematics & Science subjects. Eligible candidates from any recognized board can apply.
What placement support does the college provide?
Vaishnavi College has a dedicated Placement Cell that provides: CRT (Campus Recruitment Training), aptitude and soft skills training, resume building workshops, mock interviews, group discussion practice, and direct campus placements with 50+ hiring partners. We have achieved 500+ placements with a 95% placement rate.
What is the fee structure and are scholarships available?
Vaishnavi College offers highly affordable fees compared to other private colleges, with the added value of free industry certifications. Scholarships are available for meritorious students, economically weaker sections, and students with sports/cultural achievements. Contact our admissions office for detailed fee structure: 8074425634.
Are the certifications recognized by industry?
Yes! All our value-added certification programs are conducted through reputed training agencies and are widely recognized by the industry. Certifications in Digital Marketing, SAP, Tally, Data Analytics, AI/ML, and Web Development enhance your resume significantly and give you a competitive edge in the job market.
